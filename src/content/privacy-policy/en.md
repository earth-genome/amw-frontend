# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 13, 2026_

Amazon Mining Watch is run by Earth Genome. For anything related to your data, contact us at [info@earthgenome.org](mailto:info@earthgenome.org).

## What we collect

- **Data and code access form.** To access the data or code, we ask for your name, email and organization. You can also tell us your type of organization, your country and how you plan to use the data. We also record which link you asked for, the site language and the date.
- **Usage data.** We use Google Analytics and Hotjar to see how the site is used, e.g. which pages are visited. This can include your approximate location, device and browser.
- **Error reports.** We use Sentry to detect technical errors. Error reports can include technical details about your browser.
- **Your browser.** We save a small note in your browser's local storage to remember that you've already filled in the form, so you won't be asked again.

## Why we collect it

We use the form to understand who uses Amazon Mining Watch and how, so we can improve it and report on its impact. We only email you about data updates if you ticked the box saying we can. Usage data and error reports help us keep the site working well.

## Sharing and storage

Form responses are stored in our content management system and are only accessible to the Amazon Mining Watch team. We never sell your information. We may share aggregate, anonymized statistics (for example, the number of users by type of organization).

We keep form responses for as long as they are useful for these purposes, and no longer than five years.

## Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent to receive emails, at any time by writing to [info@earthgenome.org](mailto:info@earthgenome.org). Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as Brazil's LGPD or the EU's GDPR.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
